# Pocket Mania: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Pocket Mania is a racing game for iPhone. This policy says what the app does
with your data.

## What we collect

Pocket Mania has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
and no crash reporting. We run no servers. Online play uses Apple's Game
Center and Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), and only when you are signed in to
Game Center on your iPhone. If you are not, or you decline, the game plays
offline and sends nothing.

When you are signed in, the app sends:

- **Your Game Center player ID and nickname**, with your runs and with what
  you are driving, so others see whose time and ghost it is.
- **Your best runs**: for each track (the game's and the ones you build),
  your best time, the date, the track's id (a random code the game gives
  each track; not its name), a code for the track's layout and the game's
  physics version (so a run is only compared on the same track), and the
  run's steering, gas and brake inputs (what the game needs to replay your
  ghost) and the car skin you chose (so your ghost wears it on other
  players' phones). They are stored in the game's public iCloud database, where
  other players of Pocket Mania can download them. The game's leaderboards
  are made from these runs on each phone; there is no other leaderboard.
- **Your region code** (for example SE) from your phone's language
  settings, shown as your flag next to your name in online lobbies.
- **What you are driving**: while you race one of the game's tracks, its
  id and the time, so your Game Center friends can see "Anna is driving Tangle"
  and join you. It is overwritten each time and counts as old after two
  minutes.
- **Friends on the same track**: while you drive one of the game's tracks,
  your Game Center friends can join your race without a prompt, and when a
  friend drives the same track at the same time your phones connect by
  themselves; your run so far, your inputs while you drive and your car skin
  then go to that friend's phone through Game Center (only friends: anyone
  else is disconnected). They are not stored.
- **Notices to friends, and to the record holder**: when your new best time
  passes the best time of a Game Center friend on a track, or takes the
  fastest time in the world on a track from another player (friend or not),
  the app saves a short notice for that player in the game's public iCloud
  database. It holds your player
  ID and nickname, the friend's player ID, the track's id and its layout
  code, and the text "Your nickname just beat your time on Tangle (-0.32s)",
  with the date. Apple then sends it to that player as a notification. A
  **challenge** you send a friend is a notice of the same kind, with the
  text "Your nickname challenges you on Tangle". At most one of each goes to each player per track per hour. Your friend's phone
  deletes it once it has been read. Notices that are never read stay until
  the next one for the same friend and track replaces them, or until you
  ask us to delete them.
- **Friends by challenge link**: when you open a player's challenge link, they
  become your friend in the game (kept on your phone), and a notice like the
  ones above tells them, with your nickname, so you become theirs too.
- **Tracks you build**: when you first drive a track you built to the
  finish (a Test drive or in Solo), the track (its blocks, name, mood and
  id), your fastest time on it, and your player ID and nickname are saved in
  the game's public iCloud database, so other players of Pocket Mania see it
  in Community and can download and race it. A faster finish, or a finish
  after you change the track, replaces it. Tracks you have not finished stay
  on your phone. Send to a friend also saves a notice for that friend
  ("Your nickname sent you a track: Tangle"), as above. To have a track
  removed, write to the contact address below.
- **Ghost links**: when you tap Challenge a friend after a race, Challenge for a
  friend, or Send link in the track editor, that run (your
  inputs, its time, the track's id and content hash, your car skin, your
  player ID and nickname, and, for a track that did not come with the game,
  the track itself as in a track link) is saved in the public iCloud
  database under a random id, so whoever opens the link can race it. It is
  kept even after you drive faster; to have one removed, write to the
  contact address below.
- **Stars**: when you star a track you finished, your player ID, the track's
  id and the date are saved in the public iCloud database, so the track's
  star count can be shown; taking the star back deletes it.
- **Reports**: when you report a shared track, the app saves your player ID,
  the track's id, a short reason and the date in the public iCloud
  database. A track several players report is hidden until we check it.
  Blocking a player is kept on your phone only.
- **Notifications**: the app asks before it sends you notifications, after
  your first challenge or the first time you beat a friend, never at launch.
  If you allow them, your phone signs up with Apple's iCloud to be told
  about notices addressed to you (no address book, no location). You can
  turn them off in the game's Settings > Notifications or in iOS Settings.
The app reads your Game Center friends list on your phone (iOS asks you
first) to show your friends' times and ghosts and to challenge them; it does
not send the list anywhere. Other players' runs that the app downloads are
checked on your phone and used only to show their ghosts and times.

We (the developer) do not sell or share any of this, and use it for
nothing but the game's online features. Apple's own privacy policy covers
Game Center and iCloud.

## What stays on your phone

Your best times, replays and game settings are saved inside the app on your
phone, and deleting the app deletes them. Runs you uploaded stay in the
public database (a newer best on the same track replaces yours); to have
yours removed, write to the contact address below with your Game Center
nickname.

## Sharing and Photos

When you tap Share, the app makes a video of your replay and opens the
iPhone's share sheet. You choose where the video goes (for example Messages
or Save Video); the app sends it nowhere by itself. Save Video asks for
permission to add the video to your Photos library. The app cannot see or
read your photos.

## Ghost links

Challenge a friend on the finish screen opens the iPhone's share sheet with a link to
the run you just drove; you choose who gets it. The link carries the ghost's
random id and, for the page that opens it on a phone without the game, the
track's name, your nickname and your time, all after the "#", which browsers
never send to a server: our ghost page
(https://n1mda.github.io/pocketmania-privacy/g/) reads it in the browser only,
stores nothing and sends nothing; its buttons open the game or the App Store.

## Track links

Before 19 November 2026, Send link in the track editor put the whole track
(its blocks, name, mood, your time and your nickname) inside a link and
opened the iPhone's share sheet; you chose who got it. It now sends a ghost
link (above), and those older links still open. The track is in the part of the link after
the "#", which browsers never send to a server: our track page
(https://n1mda.github.io/pocketmania-privacy/t/) reads it in the browser only,
stores nothing and sends nothing. A friend who opens the link in the game
gets a copy of the track on their phone.

## Microphone and camera

The app does not use the microphone or the camera.

## Children

The app has no accounts of its own. Online play follows the Game Center
settings on the phone, including the restrictions parents set in Screen
Time (Game Center > Multiplayer Games, Adding Friends).

same time connects without a prompt. 14 November 2026: shared tracks (a link
you send yourself; Send to a friend and Show in Browse, which store the
track with your nickname in the public iCloud database), reports of shared
tracks, and the track page that opens a link. 15 November 2026: a track you
build is published when you first drive it to the finish (no Show in Browse
step any more), and stars on tracks. 19 November 2026: ghost links
(Challenge a friend after a race: the run saved in the public iCloud database for
whoever opens the link) and the ghost page. 22 November 2026: the player
whose world-best time on a track you beat gets the notice too, friend or not.
23 November 2026: opening a challenge link makes you and its sender friends in the game.
