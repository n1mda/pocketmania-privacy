# Pocket Mania: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Pocket Mania is a racing game for iPhone. This policy says what the app does
with your data.

## What we collect

Pocket Mania has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
and no crash reporting. We run no servers. Online play uses Apple's Game
Center and Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), and only when you are signed in to
Game Center on your iPhone. If you are not, or you decline, the game plays
offline and sends nothing.

When you are signed in, the app sends:

- **Your Game Center player ID and nickname**, with your runs and with what
  you are driving, so others see whose time and ghost it is.
- **Your best runs**: for each track (the game's and the ones you build),
  your best time, the date, the track's id (a random code the game gives
  each track; not its name), a code for the track's layout and the game's
  physics version (so a run is only compared on the same track), and the
  run's steering, gas and brake inputs (what the game needs to replay your
  ghost). They are stored in the game's public iCloud database, where
  other players of Pocket Mania can download them. The game's leaderboards
  are made from these runs on each phone; there is no other leaderboard.
- **Your region code** (for example SE) from your phone's language
  settings, shown as your flag next to your name in online lobbies.
- **What you are driving**: while you race one of the game's tracks, its
  id and the time, so your Game Center friends can see "Anna is driving Tangle"
  and join you. It is overwritten each time and counts as old after two
  minutes.
- **Live races**: when you race a friend live, the track's id and your
  inputs go to that friend's phone through Game Center while you drive.
  They are not stored.

The app reads your Game Center friends list on your phone (iOS asks you
first) to show your friends' times and ghosts and to invite them; it does
not send the list anywhere. Other players' runs that the app downloads are
checked on your phone and used only to show their ghosts and times.

We (the developer) do not sell or share any of this, and use it for
nothing but the game's online features. Apple's own privacy policy covers
Game Center and iCloud.

## What stays on your phone

Your best times, replays and game settings are saved inside the app on your
phone, and deleting the app deletes them. Runs you uploaded stay in the
public database (a newer best on the same track replaces yours); to have
yours removed, write to the contact address below with your Game Center
nickname.

## Sharing and Photos

When you tap Share, the app makes a video of your replay and opens the
iPhone's share sheet. You choose where the video goes (for example Messages
or Save Video); the app sends it nowhere by itself. Save Video asks for
permission to add the video to your Photos library. The app cannot see or
read your photos.

## Microphone and camera

The app does not use the microphone or the camera.

## Children

The app has no accounts of its own. Online play follows the Game Center
settings on the phone, including the restrictions parents set in Screen
Time (Game Center > Multiplayer Games, Adding Friends).
